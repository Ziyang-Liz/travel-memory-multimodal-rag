# Raw Travel Memory Notes

The first city was Auckland. We first went to Sierra Café for brunch, where I ordered French toast [photo of the French toast]. It was extremely delicious. The sweetness of the condensed milk blended perfectly into the cake. After that, we walked to Albert Park. Four of us took a group photo in front of the fountain in the park [group photo of four people in front of the fountain, everyone looking very happy].

Another attraction was Mount Victoria. We walked a long distance from the city to the harbour, then took a ferry to reach the Devonport area. I heard that Mount Victoria is a great place to watch the sunset, but we were all too tired at that time, so we only planned to go up, enjoy the view briefly, and then return to the hotel. Although it is a small mountain, the climb was still quite tiring. During the climb, we also took a shortcut through a small path. We walked up a very steep slope to save time, but during this process, my friend accidentally damaged their phone screen. At that moment, we decided that next time we should not do such a risky activity. When we finally reached the top of Mount Victoria, the whole city of Auckland was in view. I opened my arms and took a photo with Auckland city behind me [photo of me standing with open arms].

The next attraction was Hobbiton Movie Set. We woke up very early and took a three-hour ride to reach the filming location of The Hobbit. A tour guide led us through the whole visit and explained the connection between The Hobbit films and the filming set. I took a photo with one of the small Hobbit houses [photo with a small Hobbit house]. I have to say that the small houses were really cute. We also entered one of the Hobbit houses and looked around inside. There was a very cute children’s bunk bed [photo of the children’s bunk bed]. At the end of the Hobbiton visit, I drank a ginger beer from a cute brown cup. It tasted quite good [photo of me holding the ginger beer]. On the way back to Auckland city, the driver found some wild New Zealand feijoas by the roadside and picked some for us to taste [photo of a small pile of feijoas]. They tasted sour and sweet, and I liked them. In the evening, we had Indian food in Auckland city. The butter chicken curry with garlic naan was extremely delicious [photo of butter chicken curry and garlic naan].

The second city was Queenstown. We first went to the lakeside area in Queenstown and ate a highly rated local taco. It was juicy and tasted really good [photo of me holding the taco]. After that, we took the Skyline gondola to reach a higher viewpoint where we could overlook Queenstown. I took a photo with my good friend inside the gondola on the way up [photo with my friend in the gondola]. When we reached the top, the view looking down was wide and beautiful [photo of the view overlooking Queenstown]. There was also a luge activity on the mountain [photo of the luge activity]. It felt a little exciting. While waiting in line for the luge, my down jacket was accidentally torn. I suddenly came up with the idea of covering the torn part with bandages in a cross shape. After I put them on, it actually looked quite unique [photo of two bandages on the down jacket]. That night in Queenstown, I had cream pasta with a lot of basil leaves. I really liked the texture and flavour [photo of the pasta].

Another attraction near Queenstown was Milford Sound. Milford Sound is still quite far from Queenstown, so we left in the morning and took about a four-hour ride to get there. On the way, we stopped at Fiordland National Park for a short visit. We took a photo there with the mountains behind us while standing on the grass [photo of us standing on grass with mountains in the background]. After arriving at Milford Sound, we took a boat and experienced the beauty of the fjord landscape at close range [photo of Milford Sound]. When we returned to Queenstown in the evening, we ate the famous Fergburger and donuts from the same place. Both were extremely delicious, but I forgot to take photos of the food. I only took a photo of the Fergburger sign [photo of the Fergburger sign].

After that, we took a bus from Queenstown to Christchurch. We first passed Aoraki / Mount Cook. In April, the peak of Mount Cook was already covered with snow [photo of the snow on Mount Cook]. I heard that the glacier walk at Mount Cook is very famous, but we did not have enough time. After having a meal at Mount Cook, we continued our journey. The cream mushroom soup I had at Mount Cook was quite good. Then we passed the Church of the Good Shepherd at Lake Tekapo. From there, the reflection on the lake looked even bluer than the sky. I took a photo there that I was very satisfied with, and in the photo I was smiling very brightly [photo of me smiling brightly at the Church of the Good Shepherd].

The next city was Christchurch. We arrived in Christchurch at night, so we first looked for a place to have dinner. On the way to find dinner, we saw the “arch” in Christchurch. The arch looked quite local and atmospheric at night [night photo of the Christchurch arch]. That evening, we decided to have dinner at a western restaurant in Christchurch city centre called Amazonita. Four of us ordered cream beef pasta, spicy shrimp pasta, small squid, and small crispy balls. Every dish tasted very good. After dinner, we walked slowly back to the hotel in the cool breeze, which felt very relaxing and pleasant.

For brunch the next day, we chose a highly rated restaurant in Christchurch called Unknown Chapter. I ordered avocado toast, and it tasted good. The four of us ordered different dishes and shared them with each other. In the end, I still thought my avocado toast was the best [photo of the four dishes placed together]. After brunch, we went on a city walk in Christchurch. When we reached Victoria Square, I felt a bit tired. My two good friends tried to lift me up and carry me, and I was really lifted up. The three of us were all laughing. They said I was very heavy, hahaha [photo of me being lifted by two friends]. There were also many trams in Christchurch city [casual photo of a tram moving along the street]. We also saw the famous Christchurch attraction known as the “floating house” [photo of the floating house].

Finally, we walked to Christchurch Botanic Gardens. At the entrance of the Botanic Gardens, there was a very beautiful fountain. I took a lively photo with the beautiful fountain, and I felt very happy. I was also very satisfied with this photo [photo of me with the fountain at Christchurch Botanic Gardens]. Inside the Botanic Gardens, I also photographed leaves falling from the trees, which made me feel that autumn had truly arrived [photo of autumn leaves falling in the wind]. While waiting at Christchurch Airport, I noticed that the toilets there showed respect for gender diversity [photo of the toilet sign showing male, female, and all-gender options].
