# Auckland and Hobbiton Travel Memories

## Sierra Café
In Auckland, we first went to Sierra Café for brunch. I ordered French toast. I thought it was extremely delicious because the sweetness of condensed milk blended into the cake very well.

## Albert Park
After brunch, we walked to Albert Park in Auckland. Four of us took a group photo in front of the fountain, and everyone looked very happy.

## Mount Victoria / Takarunga
We walked a long distance from the city to the harbour and took a ferry to the Devonport area to visit Mount Victoria / Takarunga. It is known as a good place for sunset views, but we were already tired and only wanted to briefly enjoy the view before going back to the hotel. The climb was tiring. We also took a steep shortcut, but during the climb my friend's phone screen was accidentally damaged. We decided that next time we should not choose such risky shortcuts. When we finally reached the top, Auckland city could be seen clearly. I opened my arms and took a photo with the whole city view.

## Hobbiton Movie Set
We woke up very early and took a three-hour ride to Hobbiton Movie Set. A guide led us through the movie set and explained the connection between the Hobbit films and the filming location. I took a photo with one of the small Hobbit houses, and I thought the houses were very cute. We also visited the inside of one Hobbit house, where a children's bunk bed looked especially cute. At the end of the visit, I drank ginger beer from a cute brown cup.

## Feijoa and Auckland Indian Dinner
On the way back to Auckland city, the driver found wild New Zealand feijoa fruit by the roadside and picked some for us to try. The taste was sour and sweet. At night, we ate Indian food in Auckland city. The butter chicken curry with garlic naan was extremely delicious.
