# Christchurch Travel Memories

## Dinner at Amazonita and Night Walk
We arrived in Christchurch at night and looked for dinner first. On the way, we saw an arch-like landmark in the city at night, which gave me a local feeling. We had dinner at a western restaurant called Amazonita in the city centre. Four of us ordered cream beef pasta, spicy shrimp pasta, small squid, and small crispy balls. Every dish tasted very good. After dinner, we walked slowly back to the hotel in the cool wind, and it felt very relaxing.

## Unknown Chapter Brunch
The next day, we chose a highly rated brunch restaurant in Christchurch called Unknown Chapter Coffee Roasters. I ordered avocado toast. The taste was good. Four of us ordered different dishes and tried each other's food. In the end, I still thought my avocado toast was the best.

## Christchurch City Walk
After brunch, we walked around Christchurch city. At Victoria Square, I felt a bit tired. My two good friends tried to lift me up, and I was really lifted. All three of us laughed, and they joked that I was heavy. I also noticed many Christchurch trams. We saw a famous landmark that looked like a floating or suspended house.

## Christchurch Botanic Gardens and Airport
Finally, we walked to Christchurch Botanic Gardens. There was a very beautiful fountain at the entrance, and I took a lively photo with it. I felt very happy and really liked the photo. In the gardens, I also photographed falling leaves, which made me feel that autumn had really arrived. While waiting at Christchurch Airport, I noticed that the toilet signs respected gender diversity, showing male, female, and all-gender access.
