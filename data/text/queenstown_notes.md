# Queenstown, Milford Sound, Mount Cook and Lake Tekapo Travel Memories

## Queenstown Lakefront and Skyline
In Queenstown, we first ate a highly rated taco near the lake. It was juicy and tasted very good. Then we took the Skyline Queenstown Gondola to a high viewpoint overlooking Queenstown. I took a photo with my good friend inside the gondola. From the top, the landscape looked wide and beautiful, with the lake and mountains below.

## Skyline Luge and Dinner
At the top of Skyline Queenstown, we played luge. It felt a little exciting. While queueing for the luge, my down jacket was accidentally torn. I used two bandages to cover the hole in a cross shape, and it looked surprisingly unique. That night in Queenstown, I ate cream pasta with many basil leaves. I really liked the texture.

## Milford Sound and Fiordland National Park
Milford Sound was still a distance from Queenstown. We left in the morning and took about four hours to arrive. On the way, we stopped at Fiordland National Park and took photos with the mountains behind us. At Milford Sound, we took a boat and experienced the beauty of the fjord landscape closely.

## Fergburger
When we returned to Queenstown at night, we ate the famous Fergburger and also had donuts from the same place. Both were extremely delicious, but I forgot to take photos of the food and only took a photo of the Fergburger sign.

## Aoraki / Mount Cook and Lake Tekapo
On the bus from Queenstown to Christchurch, we first passed Aoraki / Mount Cook. In April, the mountain peak already had snow. I heard the glacier walk there is famous, but we did not have enough time. After a meal at Mount Cook, we continued the trip. The cream mushroom soup there was good. Later we passed the Church of the Good Shepherd at Lake Tekapo. The lake reflection looked even bluer than the sky. I took a photo there that I really liked because I was smiling brightly.
