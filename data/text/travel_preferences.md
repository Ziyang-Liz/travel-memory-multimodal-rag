# Travel Preferences

I prefer scenic outdoor places, beaches, riverside walks, lake views, mountain views, city viewpoints, and iconic landmarks. I like places suitable for photography and walking. I also enjoy memorable food experiences, especially brunch, pasta, curry, and local specialties.

I prefer travel activities that feel relaxing, beautiful, and meaningful. I like travelling with friends and keeping funny or emotional memories. I do not like overly risky shortcuts or activities that may cause accidents. I can enjoy exciting activities such as luge, but I prefer them to be safe.
